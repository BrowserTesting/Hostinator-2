About HectorCare

HectorCare was founded in 2019 out of sheer frustration. When Mark — one of our founders — got his first Pacman frog, he discovered that reliable English husbandry guides were almost non-existent. Information was scattered across forums, often contradictory, and written by anonymous users with no verifiable experience. He decided to change that.

Today, HectorCare is a four-person team of dedicated hobbyists and one veterinarian specialising in exotic animals. Between us we care for over 30 frogs of different species — from tiny Ranitomeya to 100-gram Ceratophrys. Every article we publish is based on personal experience or direct consultation with a breeder or vet. We do not copy-paste translated content — we write in plain English for keepers who shop at PetSmart and buy terrariums on Amazon.

We do not earn money by recommending specific products. All equipment and food reviews are conducted independently, and affiliate links are always clearly labelled. Our only goal is for more pet frogs to live long, healthy lives in good conditions.
