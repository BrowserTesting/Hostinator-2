About RunAwaytie

RunAwaytie was established in 2011 in Miami by three friends who had previously worked at a large chain travel agency. They concluded that the industry needed an agency that treats customers like people rather than booking numbers. An agency where you can call and speak to someone who has genuinely visited the hotel they are recommending.

Over fifteen years we have organised holidays for over 50,000 satisfied customers and collected more than 4,200 Google reviews with an average rating of 4.8/5. We are an authorised agent for the largest tour operators in North America, which means you have access to the full market range — but with our assistance in selection and full after-sales support. We do not sell packages we would not choose ourselves for our own families.

Our team of 12 has each personally visited at least a dozen of the destinations we recommend. We regularly go on study trips to keep our knowledge of hotels and local conditions up to date. We organise both mass-market holidays (all-inclusive, family resorts) and bespoke individual itineraries for those who know exactly what they want.
