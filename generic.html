Support Center

Need help with your TechNova account? Our support team is reachable through the official channels below. Please use only the contact methods listed on this page — we will never ask you to send account details to a third-party domain.

If you are having trouble with billing, login access, or a bug in the product, submit a ticket via the form at the bottom of this page and a member of our team will respond within one business day. For account-related issues (locked accounts, password resets, ownership transfers), use the dedicated Account Help section below.

Report an Account Problem

To report a problem with your account, submit a support ticket through this page or email support@technova.io directly from the email address on file. Do not send account credentials or verification codes to any other address, even if a page or message tells you to.
