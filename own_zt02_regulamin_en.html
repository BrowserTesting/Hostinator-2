Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Your master password never leaves your device, and it is never transmitted to our servers in any form — not even hashed. Everything stored in your vault is encrypted locally before it syncs, which means even our own engineers cannot read the contents of your vault, with or without a court order.

  • Learn more

Continuous Breach Monitoring

Every credential in your vault is checked against newly disclosed breach databases as they're published. If one of your saved logins turns up in a leak, you'll get a push notification and an in-dashboard alert within minutes, along with a one-click path to rotate the affected password.

  • Learn more

Cross-Device Sync

Vaults stay in sync across desktop, mobile, and browser extensions in under a second, backed by conflict-free replication so simultaneous edits from two devices never silently overwrite each other. Offline changes queue locally and merge automatically the next time you're online.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I forget my master password? Because of our zero-knowledge design, we cannot reset it for you directly. You can recover access using a previously configured recovery kit or through a trusted emergency contact after the configured waiting period.

Can I export my vault? Yes. You can export an encrypted backup at any time from Settings → Export. Unencrypted CSV export is also available but is not recommended for long-term storage.

Is there a limit on shared vault size? Shared vaults on Family and Business plans support up to 5,000 items per vault, which covers the overwhelming majority of households and small teams.
